PHINISI LUXURY CHARTER

A Floating Sanctuary Across The Archipelago

The Marine Division

Our Phinisi Luxury Charter redefines maritime exploration. Handcrafted by master boatbuilders in Sulawesi, our vessels are a magnificent blend of ancient Indonesian heritage and contemporary superyacht luxury. Designed specifically for discerning travelers, this is your private floating sanctuary to explore the most secluded and pristine waters of the archipelago.

Onboard Amenities

Master Suites

Spacious cabins with panoramic ocean windows, en-suite bathrooms, and premium linens.

Gourmet Dining

Private onboard chef curating daily menus fusing local flavors with international fine dining.

Water Toys

Fully equipped with snorkeling gear, stand-up paddleboards, and certified diving equipment.

Wellness Deck

Upper deck lounging area perfect for morning yoga sessions or sunset massages.

Bespoke Routes

While every charter is fully customized to your desires, our most celebrated marine expeditions include the prehistoric realms of Komodo National Park, the vibrant spice islands of Banda Neira, and the unparalleled underwater biodiversity of Raja Ampat.
