Flores, NTT 4 Days / 3 Nights

Luxury Sailing Komodo

Overview

Embark on a breathtaking journey through the pristine waters of the Komodo Archipelago aboard a private luxury phinisi. Witness ancient dragons, swim with manta rays, and relax on untouched pink sand beaches.

Daily Itinerary

Arrive at Labuan Bajo airport where our concierge will greet you. Transfer to the harbor and board your private yacht. Sail to Kelor Island for a short trek and panoramic views, followed by sunset cocktails on Kalong Island while watching thousands of flying foxes.

Wake up early for a sunrise trek at Padar Island. Enjoy breakfast on board before sailing to the famous Pink Beach for snorkeling in crystal-clear waters.
