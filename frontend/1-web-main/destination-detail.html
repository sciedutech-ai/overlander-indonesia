The Experience

Embark on a breathtaking journey through the pristine waters of the Komodo Archipelago aboard a private luxury phinisi. Witness ancient dragons, swim with manta rays, and relax on untouched pink sand beaches. This bespoke expedition is crafted for those who seek the ultimate marine adventure without compromising absolute comfort.

Journey Highlights

  • Private luxury wooden Phinisi charter
  • Full-board gourmet meals by a private chef
  • Guided trekking to Padar Island sunrise
  • Snorkeling with Manta Rays & Turtles
  • VVIP Airport transfers in Labuan Bajo

Daily Itinerary

Arrive at Labuan Bajo airport where our concierge will greet you. Transfer to the harbor and board your private yacht. Sail to Kelor Island for a short trek and panoramic views, followed by sunset cocktails on Kalong Island while watching thousands of flying foxes.

Wake up early for a sunrise trek at Padar Island. Enjoy breakfast on board before sailing to the famous Pink Beach for snorkeling in crystal-clear waters and a private beach picnic setup.

Guided trek in Komodo National Park to encounter the prehistoric dragons. In the afternoon, drift snorkel at Manta Point and relax at the crescent-shaped Taka Makassar sandbar.
