OUR STORY

The Manifesto

Redefining Rugged Luxury

Overlander Indonesia was born from a pure vision: to bridge the untamed spirit of wild adventure with the absolute highest standards of living comfort. We believe that exploring the most remote destinations in the archipelago should never mean compromising the luxury of your privacy.

We design every expedition as a personalized masterpiece. From the roar of a 4x4 engine traversing ancient, arid savannas, to the profound silence of a night spent on the deck of a luxury Phinisi amidst the pristine waters of Flores, we ensure your journey is paved with impeccable service and flawless attention to detail.

The Vision

To be the Premier Land and Air-Based Luxury Private Expedition Pioneer in Southeast Asia.

Redefining the boundaries of premium adventure by crafting a travel ecosystem that prioritizes absolute safety, freedom of exploration, and boundless luxury.

Noble Purpose

Preserving the Authentic Soul of the Archipelago and Empowering Local Communities.

We open the gates to Indonesia's deepest, untouched beauty to the world, while actively delivering direct economic empowerment and preserving cultural and ecological sustainability for future generations.

Bespoke Pillars

The three main pillars of our boundless comfort services.

Bespoke Overland 4x4

Fully equipped luxury tactical fleets ready to conquer extreme, remote terrains without ever compromising first-class cabin comfort.

Phinisi Luxury Charter

Navigate the magical Indonesian archipelago aboard legendary wooden vessels, complete with five-star amenities and a dedicated private crew.

Bespoke Aviation

Maximize your time and witness breathtaking aerial landscapes with private helicopters and jets chartered directly to your exclusive destinations.

Our Commitment

Uncompromising Standards

01 / Aviation-Standard Safety

Every vehicle, crew member, and satellite communication system operates under strict, uncompromising risk mitigation protocols.

02 / Integrated Eco-Luxury

We minimize our expedition's carbon footprint by maintaining a zero-plastic-waste policy and utilizing sustainable, eco-friendly supplies.

Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Overlander Indonesia. By booking an expedition with us, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms and conditions.

1. Booking & Payments

A non-refundable deposit of 30% is required to secure your bespoke expedition. The remaining balance must be paid in full 60 days prior to the departure date.

2. Cancellations

Cancellations made within 30 days of the departure date are subject to a 100% cancellation fee. We highly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance.

3. Itinerary Changes

Due to the remote nature of our expeditions, itineraries are subject to change based on weather, safety, and local conditions. Our team will ensure the highest standard of alternatives.

Privacy Policy

At Overlander Indonesia, we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients. This policy outlines how we handle your personal information.

1. Information Collection

We collect information necessary to craft your bespoke journey, including full names, contact details, dietary requirements, and medical conditions strictly for safety purposes.

2. Data Security

All personal data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized personnel involved in executing your expedition.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell your data. Information is only shared with trusted local partners (e.g., flight operators, private villas) strictly to facilitate your travel arrangements.

Cookie Policy

To provide a seamless and personalized browsing experience, Overlander Indonesia utilizes cookies on our website.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us understand how you interact with our digital platform.

2. How We Use Them

We use essential cookies for website functionality and analytical cookies to improve our bespoke offerings based on user interactions.

3. Managing Preferences

You can choose to disable analytical cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect the premium experience of our website.
